## Score Breakdown

| # | Category | Max Points | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Application & Action** | 10 | Did the client complete the lesson's action steps? |
| 2 | **Reading & Listening** | 10 | Combined repetitions — target is 6 total |
| 3 | **Best Idea** | 10 | 5 pts for identifying the idea + 5 pts for applying it |
| 4 | **Business Goals Attainment** | 10 | Progress on business/professional goals set last session |
| 5 | **Personal Goals Attainment** | 10 | Progress on personal goals set last session |
| 6 | **Intangible Goal Attainment** | 10 | Movement on the intangible score toward the stated goal |
| 7 | **Satisfaction from Chapter** | 40 | How relevant and impactful was this lesson to their situation? |
| | **TOTAL** | **100** | |

## How to Score Each Category

### 1. Application & Action (10 pts)
- **10** — All action steps completed
- **7–9** — Most completed (80%+)
- **5–6** — About half completed
- **1–4** — Attempted but incomplete
- **0** — Not done

### 2. Reading & Listening (10 pts)
- Formula: **(actual reps ÷ 6) × 10**, rounded to nearest whole number
- Example: 3 reps = 5 pts | 6 reps = 10 pts | 2 reps = 3 pts

### 3. Best Idea (10 pts)
- **5 pts** — Client identified a meaningful idea from the lesson
- **+ 5 pts** — Client applied (or has a concrete plan to apply) it
- **3 pts** — Idea identified but vague application
- **0** — No idea identified

### 4. Business Goals Attainment (10 pts)
- Self-assessed against goals set at the end of the previous session
- **10** — All business goals achieved
- **5–7** — Partial progress
- **0** — No progress

### 5. Personal Goals Attainment (10 pts)
- Same structure as Business Goals
- **10** — All personal goals achieved
- **5–7** — Partial progress
- **0** — No progress

### 6. Intangible Goal Attainment (10 pts)
- Based on movement in the intangible score (from beginning to end of session, or since last session)
- **10** — Reached or exceeded the stated intangible goal
- **5–8** — Positive movement toward goal
- **0** — No movement or regression

### 7. Satisfaction from Chapter (40 pts)
- Self-reported by client: "How relevant and impactful was this lesson to your current situation?"
- This is the most subjective and highest-weighted category
- Facilitator guides the discussion; client scores themselves
- **36–40** — Deeply relevant; breakthrough insights
- **25–35** — Relevant with actionable takeaways
- **15–24** — Somewhat relevant
- **0–14** — Limited relevance to current situation

## Score Benchmarks

| Total Score | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| 90–100 | Exceptional engagement — celebrate and anchor |
| 75–89 | Strong — acknowledge and build momentum |
| 60–74 | Good — identify what held back full engagement |
| 40–59 | Moderate — explore blockers; adjust approach if needed |
| Below 40 | Low — significant follow-up required; check in on motivation |
