### 📏 PFS Scoring Legend

| Category | Points | How to Score |
|---|---|---|
| **Application & Action** | 10 | Did the client complete the action steps in M021? Full completion = 10. Partial = 5. None = 0. |
| **Reading & Listening** | 10 | Combined repetitions target is 6. Score = (reps / 6) × 10, rounded. E.g., 3 reps = 5 pts. |
| **Best Idea** | 10 | 5 pts for identifying the idea + 5 pts for having applied (or concretely planning to apply) it. |
| **Business Goals Attainment** | 10 | Self-assessed vs. goals set last session. Full attainment = 10; none = 0. |
| **Personal Goals Attainment** | 10 | Self-assessed vs. goals set last session. Full attainment = 10; none = 0. |
| **Intangible Goal Attainment** | 10 | Based on intangible score movement. Positive movement toward goal = 10; no movement = 0. |
| **Satisfaction / Relevance** | 40 | How relevant and impactful was this chapter to the client's current situation? Self-reported. 0–40. |
